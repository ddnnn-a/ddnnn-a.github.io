Questions, answered.

Can I try it before buying?

Yes. Everything works free for the first 24 hours after your first launch — no license needed. After that, a one-time $9.99 license unlocks it for good.

Which Macs are supported?

macOS 14 Sonoma or later, on Apple silicon and Intel. The app is signed and notarized by Apple.

Why does it need accessibility permission?

Moving and resizing other apps' windows is only possible through macOS's accessibility API. That's the only thing the permission is used for.

Can I move my license to a new Mac?

A license covers 3 active Macs, and you can deactivate a device any time from your purchase portal.

What if it doesn't fit my workflow?

Ask for a refund within 14 days and you'll get one, no questions asked. Purchases are handled by Polar, our merchant of record — open your receipt email or your purchase portal to request it.

Does SidePin see what's in my windows?

No. It reads window positions and sizes so it can move them, and nothing else. No analytics, no accounts, no window contents — ever. The only thing it sends over the network is a license check.

Something's broken. Where do I get help?

Open an issue on GitHub and you'll get a reply. For anything about a payment, refund or receipt, contact Polar.
